## Description
One tap. Speak. Done.

Turn everyday spoken notes into reminders on your iPhone with ikke glem by MP. Choose Norwegian, German or English, tap the green voice button and say what you want to remember and when you want to be reminded.

For example:
• “Remind me in ten minutes.”
• “The key is in the cupboard, remind me at 655.”
• “Doctor tomorrow at 18:00, remind me at 17:41.”
• “Doctor tomorrow at eight, remind me thirty minutes before.”

You choose the reminder time. Without a separate alert time, the app reminds you at the appointment. Check the recognized text and displayed times after saving.

iPhone schedules local notifications even when the app is closed. Notification sound follows your iPhone sound, Focus and notification settings. This app is not a critical alarm.

The recognized text, messages and reminder list disappear from the screen after ten seconds. Your reminders remain saved and can be viewed again using “My reminders”.

You decide which reminders are also added to your calendar. A Google calendar can be used if its account is already configured in your iPhone calendar accounts. Addresses can be opened in Apple Maps.

Prefer typing? Enter a reminder under “Help and privacy”.

No user account, advertising or tracking. Reminders are stored on your iPhone, and the app does not store audio recordings. Apple speech recognition may require internet access and process audio on Apple servers. Calendar and Maps use the services and accounts you choose.

Do not speak or type passwords, bank account numbers, card numbers or other sensitive information.

## App Review notes
No login or demo account is required. Choose English, allow Microphone, Speech Recognition and Notifications, then tap the green voice button and say “Remind me in two minutes”. The reminder is saved after recognition completes or a short pause. Lock the device and wait for the local notification. If speech is unavailable, type the same sentence under “Help and privacy” → “Type instead”.

Norwegian Bokmål, German and English are supported. The recognized sentence, feedback and reminder list disappear after ten seconds without deleting reminders or cancelling notifications. “My reminders” shows the list again.

Calendar saving is optional for each reminder. Tap “Add to calendar” to save an event. A Google account must already be configured in iOS Calendar; there is no Google sign-in inside the app. Addresses use Apple geocoding and Apple Maps; GPS access is not requested.

Custom notification audio is bundled. iOS sound, notification and Focus settings apply. The app does not use Critical Alerts.
